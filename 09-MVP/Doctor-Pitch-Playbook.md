# Doctor Pitch Playbook - MEDOXZI Pre-Round

**Status:** current doctor-facing pitch guide  
**Updated:** 2026-08-24, session J  
**Audience:** founder, sales, product, future agents  
**Purpose:** help the team explain MEDOXZI to doctors truthfully, powerfully, and without crossing safety/regulatory boundaries.

## 1. Opening Position

Use this tone: practical, doctor-respecting, non-hype.

> Doctor sahib, main aapko AI doctor sell nahi kar raha. MEDOXZI diagnosis ya treatment recommend nahi karega. Yeh system patient ka waiting time useful banata hai: patient ki basic information, short issue description, relevant history questions, medicines, allergies aur optional reports collect karke aapke phone/tablet par ek clean brief bhejta hai before patient room mein aaye.

Short version:

> Aapka clinical decision aapka rahega. System sirf patient ki story ko organize karke aap tak pehle pohanchata hai.

## 2. The Core Problem To Name

Doctors already know this pain. Say it simply:

- OPD mein doctor ka valuable time aksar diagnosis se pehle **history lene, repeat questions poochne, medicines/allergies samajhne aur reports dekhne** mein chala jata hai.
- Patient waiting area mein already time spend kar raha hota hai. Us waqt basic intake ho sakta hai.
- Jab doctor patient ko dekhe, doctor ko blank start nahi karna chahiye.
- Patient ko har follow-up par apni kahani phir se zero se nahi batani chahiye.

## 3. What The System Does In The First Version

Say this as a direct product walkthrough:

1. Staff patient ko register karta hai aur encounter/token create hota hai.
2. Patient ya staff basic personal information confirm karta hai.
3. Patient 2-3 lines mein apna issue likhta hai, apni zubaan mein.
4. System us issue ke hisaab se Lead-Doctor-approved basic questions poochta hai.
5. Patient medicines, allergies, past history aur current symptoms answer karta hai.
6. Agar previous reports hain to attach ho jati hain; agar nahi hain to bhi flow complete hai.
7. Doctor ke tablet/phone par short brief ready hota hai before consultation.
8. Doctor report khud dekhta hai, history verify karta hai, conclusion khud deta hai.

## 4. Strong Pitch Lines

### Time Saving

> Doctor sahib, aapka consultation time history typing mein waste na ho. Patient room mein aaye to aap already complaint, duration, medicines, allergies, positives, negatives aur missing questions dekh saken.

### Better First Visit

> Sabse pehle hum first-visit patients se start karenge, specially jin ke paas reports nahi hoti. Is case mein system ka value clear hota hai: patient ki story pehle se structured ho kar aap tak aa jati hai.

### Patient Code And Future History

> Har patient ek unique code/record se save hoga. Follow-up par aap naam ya code se patient ki previous visits, history, medicines, allergies, attached reports aur aapke last conclusions dekh sakenge.

Truth boundary: say "clinic record/history", not national EMR integration unless that integration is actually built and approved.

### Follow-Up Reminder

> Jab aap consultation conclude karenge, aap follow-up date enter kar sakenge. System us date ko save karega aur clinic ki taraf se patient ko follow-up se 24 hours pehle WhatsApp/Email reminder bhej sakta hai.

Truth boundary: this is a clinic-owned operational message and needs proper consent/configuration.

### Patient Care After Visit

> Agar follow-up zaroori nahi hai, clinic kuch din baad patient ko human message bhej sakti hai: "Aap ko ye issue tha, ab kaisa feel kar rahe hain? Agar masla abhi bhi hai to hum appointment schedule kar sakte hain. Agar theek hain to feedback/rating de dein."

Truth boundary: avoid medical advice in the message. Keep it as a check-in and appointment offer.

### Loyalty And Credibility

> Patients ko feel hoga ke clinic unko visit ke baad bhi remember karti hai. Yeh credibility, loyalty aur repeat visits improve kar sakta hai.

Truth boundary: say "can improve" or "helps create", not guaranteed sales growth.

### Doctor's Searchable Memory

> Aapke paas clinic ka searchable history bank banega. Kisi follow-up ya similar case ko dekhte waqt aap old visits, patient history, reports aur apne previous assessment easily search kar sakenge.

Truth boundary: similar-case search is for doctor reference and learning, not automated diagnosis.

### Clinic Announcements

> Aap selected patients ya all opted-in patients ko clinic announcements bhej sakte hain: timing change, location change, new facility, new branch, doctor availability, booking link.

Truth boundary: clinic-owned announcements only; patient can opt out where required.

### Case Study Offer

> Doctor sahib, agar aap case study ke liye ready hain, hum initial 3 months special discounted package de sakte hain. Case study mein hum aapke clinic ka workflow improvement, better follow-up process aur digital history discipline show karenge.

Truth boundary: do not promise performance numbers before measured pilot data. Do not expose patient identity.

### Future AI, Carefully

> Future mein, jab enough real-world evidence, doctor feedback, regulatory review aur validation complete ho jaye, system doctor ke liye possible differentials ya diagnosis-confirmation question lists suggest kar sakta hai. Lekin first version mein yeh visible nahi hoga.

Truth boundary: future differential/diagnosis support is gated; never pitch it as current capability.

### Research And Publications

> Agar clinic ka data digitally structured hoga, aap de-identified aggregate trends, tables aur diagrams generate kar sakenge. Future mein yeh audit, internal quality improvement, presentations ya papers ke liye useful ho sakta hai.

Truth boundary: research/papers require de-identification, consent/ethics where applicable, and no patient identity exposure.

### Growth Services

> Long term mein MEDOXZI clinic growth stack ban sakta hai: booking system, reminders, patient feedback, clinic announcements, digital marketing support, social media coordination, website/Google visibility, and future discovery channels where legally available.

Truth boundary: these are post-MVP services, not clinical claims.

## 6. Things Not To Say

Never say:

- "AI diagnosis karega."
- "AI doctor se better suggest karega."
- "Guaranteed time save hoga."
- "Clinical accuracy measured hai."
- "No red flags."
- "Patient data hum marketing ke liye use karenge."
- "Regulatory issue solved hai."
- "Reports se system automatically final conclusion nikal lega."
- "Future diagnoses current version mein available hain."

Use instead:

- "Doctor decision maker rahega."
- "System history organise karega."
- "Hum pilot mein measure karenge."
- "No clinic-approved safety rules are active until you sign them."
- "Patient communication clinic-owned hogi."
- "Regulatory/counsel review required hai."

## 7. Suggested Doctor Conversation

Founder:

> Dr. Abrar, OPD mein aapka sabse expensive time woh hota hai jab patient room mein aa kar basic history batana start karta hai. MEDOXZI ka kaam yeh hai ke patient waiting time mein woh basic history collect ho jaye.

Doctor:

> AI diagnosis karega?

Founder:

> Nahi. First version bilkul diagnosis nahi karega. Yeh patient ki information organise karega: complaint, duration, relevant answers, medicines, allergies, previous reports agar hain, aur missing questions. Aap decision khud lenge.

Doctor:

> Agar patient wrong answer de?

Founder:

> Isliye har cheez source ke sath dikhegi: patient reported, staff entered, caregiver entered, attached report, unconfirmed extraction. Aap verify karenge. Agar kuch nahi poocha gaya, system usko "No" nahi banayega; "not asked" rakhega.

Doctor:

> Mere clinic ko business value kya hai?

Founder:

> Pehli value: consultation start faster and cleaner. Doosri: follow-up discipline. Aap follow-up date save karenge, patient ko reminder ja sakta hai. Agar follow-up required nahi hai to clinic check-in message bhej sakti hai: "Ab kaisa feel kar rahe hain?" Is se patient ko lagta hai clinic genuinely care karti hai.

Doctor:

> Data ka future use?

Founder:

> Aapki clinic ka searchable digital history bank banega. Follow-up pe purani history mil jati hai. Later, de-identified aggregate tables/diagrams quality improvement ya papers ke liye useful ho sakte hain, proper consent/ethics ke sath.

Founder close:

> Hum aapse sirf yeh validate karna chahte hain: agar patient ki basic history pehle se aapke phone/tablet par aa jaye, kya aapka consultation smoother hota hai? First pilot mein hum claim nahi karenge; hum measure karenge.

## 8. Offer Structure

Truthful offer:

> Agar aap case study ke liye ready hain, hum first 3 months discounted pilot de sakte hain. In return, hum measured results, doctor feedback, patient completion rate aur workflow improvement ka de-identified case study bana sakte hain. Is se MEDOXZI ko proof milega aur aapki clinic bhi innovation-focused, patient-follow-up-focused clinic ke taur par visible hogi.

Do not offer:

- guaranteed patient growth;
- guaranteed time saving;
- guaranteed clinical improvement;
- patient-data access for MEDOXZI marketing;
- identifiable patient stories without separate explicit consent.

## 9. Product Suggestions From This Pitch

These are recommended product features to evaluate:

1. **Doctor quick templates:** doctor can save common follow-up instructions as clinic-approved templates.
2. **Follow-up risk label controlled by doctor:** `routine`, `important`, `urgent clinic follow-up`, but wording must be doctor-authored.
3. **Missed follow-up queue:** patients who did not return after a requested follow-up appear in a staff worklist.
4. **Patient recovery check-in:** a gentle check-in message after a configurable number of days, clinic-owned and opt-out aware.
5. **Rating request only after positive recovery response:** ask for rating after patient says they are better, not immediately after consultation.
6. **Doctor searchable case library:** search by symptom, diagnosis text, medication, date range, and attached-report type.
7. **Clinic announcement center:** timing/location/facility/branch messages to opted-in patients.
8. **De-identified clinic insights:** aggregate complaint mix, follow-up adherence, no-show rate, repeat-visit rate and patient feedback trends.
9. **Case study mode:** pilot dashboard designed to produce a clean before/after operational story without inventing clinical numbers.
10. **Booking handoff:** every reminder/check-in can offer a clinic-approved appointment booking link.
