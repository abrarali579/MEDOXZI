# Session D — v2.2 verification and OPS system

**Date:** 2026-08-23 · **Agent:** Claude (Cowork) · **Status:** COMPLETE
**Trigger:** founder asked for the external agent's v2.2 changes to be checked, findings logged, and a multi-agent logging system built.

---

## What I set out to do

Verify — not accept — the v2.2 change report; log the findings; build a governance system so multiple agents can work on this repository without contradicting each other; produce a handoff prompt.

## Method

Copied the repository out of the working folder into a clean Linux container and re-ran everything from scratch. Read all four new documents in full. Grepped every value the report claimed to have changed. Re-fetched the disputed regulatory article verbatim from two independent primary URLs.

**Deliberately did not** rely on the change report for any verdict.

## What I found

**Nine of ten claims verified.** The v2.2 work is good: real bug fix (UTF-8), a genuine architectural gap closed (traceable ≠ true), proper design-control artefacts (Hazard-Control-Matrix, Safety-Case), careful non-breaking rename with aliases.

**One claim did not survive.** "Resolved the 500-real-encounter sequencing issue" was written as intent into `Pilot-Plan.md` and the v2.2 direction document, but the two files that hold the gate — `Validation-Plan.md` Stage 4 and `Acceptance-Criteria.md` Gate 3 — were unchanged. The contradiction became three-way and less visible. **Fixed in this session; ADR-029.**

**And they corrected us.** Their downgrade of the localisation claim is right and our session-C claim was wrong. Permenkes 24/2022 Pasal 22(1) uses *dapat* (may), conditioned on *keterbatasan sumber daya* — it is the permissive route for outsourced storage, not a general prohibition. This is the **second** regulatory over-read by this project, which is why AGENT-PROTOCOL Rule 4 now requires quoting the article verbatim from a primary source.

## What I did NOT change, and why

- **The v2.2 architecture and documents.** They are sound. Rewriting them for style would destroy provenance and waste the next agent's time.
- **The regulatory design intent.** Still design for in-Indonesia storage — the certainty and the reasoning changed, not the conclusion.
- **The illustrative numbers in the pitch dossier.** They are correctly labelled illustrative. Logged as OT-06 so whoever prepares the pitch must replace them with measured figures or delete them.

## The lesson this session produced

Two of the three problems found here were the same failure: **a decision recorded in prose and believed to be implemented.** Once in the regulatory claim, once in the sequencing gate. That is the specific thing `_OPS/` is designed to prevent — hence Rule 1 (no claim without evidence) and Rule 2 (change, propagate, verify).
