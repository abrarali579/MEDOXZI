## The three findings that matter

1. **Health data must be stored in Indonesian data centres** (GR 28/2024). This forces in-country inference — most likely a self-hosted open-weights model in a Jakarta region — and it makes the deterministic-first architecture a compliance strategy as well as a safety one.
2. **Electronic medical records are mandatory for every clinic** (Permenkes 24/2022), with sanctions escalating to business permit revocation. The wedge becomes *compliance relief plus time saved*, not time saved alone.
3. **The device-classification argument is weaker here than in India** — no enumerated exclusion list was found. This is the first question for counsel, and the v2 removal of red flags from the MVP gives the cleanest possible starting position.
